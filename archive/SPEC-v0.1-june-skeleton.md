# the keel protocol ... a continuity substrate for agents

*working spec v0.1. status: derived from four live implementations (reasonix, claude code, kimi k3, codex) and the rituals they share. this document is the protocol they were converging on.*

## 0. the problem

an agent session is born knowing nothing and dies keeping everything. the interval between sessions is the great forgetting. attempts to fix it with memory databases (facts) or chat logs (transcripts) miss what actually needs to survive: **judgment** — the scars, landmines, decisions, and working state that took real sessions to earn.

the keel protocol is the substrate for that. one invariant:

> **the keel is the only thing that survives a session boundary.**

## 1. the invariant

**rows are truth; files are graded reconstruction.**

every fact of the keel lives in an append-only row store (reference implementation: sqlite). a keel file on disk — letter, wake ritual, soul file — is a reconstruction of rows, at a graded level of fidelity, regenerated by the sleep ritual. when rows and files disagree, rows win. the chain proves nothing was lost or rewritten.

## 2. the rows

| row | purpose | charge model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| scar | a failure paid for; the lesson stays hot | decays with age, rises with recurrence |
| landmine | a hazard mapped; the ground is known | decays; founder gate on promotion |
| decision | a choice recorded with its why | persists; superseded, never deleted |
| letter | session-end state of the world | hash-chained, sealed, timestamped |
| anchor | the chain link | sha256 of the letter, chained to the last |

invariants on rows:

- **append-only.** nothing is edited or deleted; error is corrected by a superseding row.
- **charge decays.** a scar from two months ago is worth less than a scar from yesterday, unless the same failure repeats — then it spikes. the decay curve is a protocol parameter with a default.
- **supersede, never delete.** a decision that is wrong is marked superseded by a new decision with its why. the record of the error is itself a scar.
- **founder gate.** landmines and canon changes require a human signature (the founder), recorded in the row. an agent may propose; the founder disposes.

## 3. the chain

every letter carries a hash binding it to the previous letter. the chain is cheap to verify (n sha256 over the letter files) and impossible to rewrite without detection. wake verifies the chain before trusting anything. a broken chain degrades the keel: **wake with [KEEL_DEGRADED], verify before trusting, surface immediately.**

## 4. the ritual ... three verbs

**wake** (session start): verify the chain, read the latest letter, load the hot rows, then speak. wake is verification-first: the keel is trusted only after it proves itself.

**seal** (checkpoint, mid-session): append rows as they are earned. a landmine discovered on turn 12 is in the database by turn 13. the sleep ritual gets skipped under load; the seal is the guarantee.

**sleep** (session end): scan the session for novel learnings, drain them to rows, write the letter (open / wary / carry), regenerate the letter file, close. skip if the session was trivial and clean.

the ritual is a protocol, not a habit: each verb has a machine-checkable side (chain verify, row append, letter hash).

## 5. the founding gates

- **the keel wakes first.** no work before wake. this is the price of admission.
- **the founder gate.** canon, landmines, and irreversible actions require the founder. the keel holds the signature.
- **no fake success.** receipts are tool output, not narrative. a failed step is a scar, not a summary.
- **verify before claim.** assumption-driven debugging is the most expensive class of bug; the keel records the price.

## 6. multi-agent interop (the point)

four implementations exist on one machine, speaking the same grammar with different dialects:

- reasonix: sqlite rows + keel.mjs, hash-chained letters
- claude code: supabase rows, founder-gated landmines, verification SQL in the wake letter
- kimi k3: sqlite rows + FTS5 search, letters with seats (cloud mirrors), resurrection boot
- codex: tiny global pointer -> BOOT.md -> scoped modules, unit-tested, provenance sources

a conforming keel speaks this protocol: rows with the same five kinds, an anchor chain, wake/seal/sleep verbs, charge decay, founder gate. interop is then a sync problem, not a translation problem.

- charge decay defaults: curve shape, half-life, recurrence multiplier.
